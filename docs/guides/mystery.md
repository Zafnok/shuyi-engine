# Mystery

- **Covers:** `kind/mystery`
- **Status:** planned
- **Tickets:** 2727, 2732

A case to solve: clues found, people questioned, places searched, and a
case file where the player puts them together. A module,
`shuyi-mystery`, will hold the clues, people, places and the case file
(ticket 2727), and an example and template, a short mystery (ticket
2732), writes this guide in full.

## Until then

- Its scenes are dialogue (the `shuyi-dialogue` skill) and its story can
  be a [visual novel](visual-novel.md)'s; what is new is the case file,
  which waits for the module.
- Keep what the player has found in the rules, not the screen, so a
  save and a replay hold it.
