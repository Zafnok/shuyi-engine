Three ways in, all coming soon:

- **The book.** A quick start, then a chapter for each idea the engine
  is built on: the core and its determinism, screens and skins, input
  actions, assets and formats, and the tools agents use.
- **The tutorial.** A small puzzle game built step by step, each step
  tested and playable in the browser.
- **The API docs.** Every crate's reference, generated from the code.

Until they land, the
[README](https://github.com/Zafnok/shuyi-engine#readme) says what the
engine is, and [Hello, Grid](@/examples/hello-grid.md) shows a whole
game made from its crates alone.
