This tutorial takes you from nothing to a finished small game: a
box-pushing puzzle on a grid. You walk, you push a box, you put every box
on a target, you undo a mistake, and the levels come from files.

Each step below is a whole project that builds and passes its tests with
the engine of the same day, and each plays in your browser from its page.
Every piece of code on a page comes from that step's files, so you can
always open the whole project and compare it with yours.

The [book](@/learn/book/_index.md) explains each idea on its own. Here you
use them in order, and each step links the chapter to read for more.
