This book teaches you to make games with Shuyi Engine, from installing it to putting a
finished game online. Each chapter takes one idea the engine is built on and shows it on
a small game, Moon Garden. Then you use it in the book's project: a box-pushing puzzle
you build a step at a time, from an empty folder to a game anyone can play in their
browser.

A note that only a newcomer needs says who it is for: new to programming, to making
games, to Rust or to Shuyi. The bar at the top of each page leaves out the notes you
don't need. A note you see can be hidden, and leaves a line you can click to show it
again.
