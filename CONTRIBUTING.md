# Contributing

Thanks for your interest. Shuyi Engine is at its **planning stage**: its
code is still being carved out of the game it was built for, and lands
here in one move (see [`docs/ROADMAP.md`](docs/ROADMAP.md)).

**For now:**

- **Issues and discussions are welcome.** Ideas, questions, and "I'd use
  this for…" are all useful.
- **Pull requests from outside aren't accepted yet.** The owner decides
  the contribution policy before the engine's first public release
  (tracked in the game repository's ticket 0059). That decision covers
  whether pull requests come under a Developer Certificate of Origin.

When pull requests open, they will be judged the way every change here is
judged, whoever or whatever wrote it:

- the quality gates pass (formatting, lints, tests including mutation
  testing, the license check);
- the change follows a ticket and the rules in [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md);
- the description says clearly what changed and why.

Contributions are licensed as MIT OR Apache-2.0, as the README says.
