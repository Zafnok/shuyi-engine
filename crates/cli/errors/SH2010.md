# SH2010: A number or a size is out of range

Class: content, exit code 1.

A number in the file, or the size of what it holds, is outside what it
may be: in `keymap.ron`, a repeat interval of 0 or a crank notch not 1
to 360 degrees; a map wider or taller than the most a map may be.

**How to fix it.** Bring it within the range the message gives. A map
too big is split into maps that each fit.

For example:

```text
base/data/keymap.ron:40: error[SH2010]: crank notch_degrees must be 1 to 360
```
