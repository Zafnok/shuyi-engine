# SH3017: The two worktrees are on different volumes

Class: operation, exit code 1.

`shuyi seed-target` shares the warm checkout's compiled files with the
new worktree by hard links, which take no disk of their own. A hard link
can't cross from one volume to another (from `D:` to `C:`, or to another
mounted disk), and the two worktrees are on different ones. Nothing was
written.

**How to fix it.** Skip the seed: the new worktree's first build then
compiles everything, as it would have without it. To seed next time, make
the worktree on the same volume as the main checkout.

For example:

```text
seed-target: error[SH3017]: D:/games/moon-garden/target/debug and C:/Users/you/.codex/worktrees/521a/moon-garden/target are on different volumes, and a hard link can't cross them
```
